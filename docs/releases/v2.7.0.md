## Improvement and safety behavior

Objective-exam laterality: all recognized notations (slash, word, typed, descriptive reflex, objective weakness) now share one opposite-side check, applied after source qualification. Mismatched-side findings, normal or abnormal, require NEEDS_REVIEW. Trade-off: notes that mention the opposite side in any way (negated, historical, bilateral wording, or a relative) also require review. Diagnosis-side vocabulary is unchanged.

Shared qualification removes inconsistent interpretation among recognized routes.
A normal finding on the opposite side does not establish the relevant objective
exam requirement; the product owner explicitly approved NEEDS_REVIEW rather than
NOT_READY. Matching, unsided and bilateral examination handling retains its
existing bounded semantics. Explicitly mismatched findings go to review rather
than a confident qualifying or nonqualifying interpretation.

## Remaining limitations

Literal side matching does not solve general negation, episode-aware temporality,
cross-sentence attribution, anatomical inference or all clinical-language forms.
Historical, negated, relative-attributed and mixed-side mentions can over-trigger
review. Patient/history source qualification is sentence-scoped and finite;
it does not establish general provenance. Existing neither/nor, unrelated therapy,
legacy date association and cross-sentence failures remain documented. No held-out
or real patient cases were accessed. Tests are synthetic regression evidence,
not clinical validation, production certification or evidence of payer approval.
