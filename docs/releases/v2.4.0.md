# v2.4.0 — bounded recall improvements

Engine/app version is **2.4.0**; record `schema_version` remains **2.0.0**.
This release packages exp-002 at `3004eb4`. Release preparation changes only
documentation, version bookkeeping, executable contract examples and generated
version fields; it adds no product logic. Policies, rulebook, clocks, freshness
windows, monitoring state and deployment configuration are unchanged.

## Four accepted fixes

The fixed CPB 0236 lumbar rule requires an objective motor/reflex finding in a
specific root distribution and no improvement after six weeks of qualifying
conservative therapy. Footnote 1's classes remain the target, not a vocabulary
inferred from drug names.

| Fix | Result and limits | Regression evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Whole-day qualifying courses | Isolated complete course/response statements recognize 42 days as six completed weeks; 41 days remains below threshold. Uses floor division by seven. | Iteration 2: three positives, ten negatives |
| Whole-month qualifying courses | Same isolated grammar recognizes whole months using four completed weeks per month as a conservative lower bound; one month cannot meet six weeks. No calendar-date inference. | Iteration 3: three positives, ten negatives |
| Small number words | Same grammar recognizes one through twelve; short and improved courses correctly fail. Comparative, ranged, fractional and annotated statements remain unsupported. | Iteration 4: three positives, eleven negatives |
| Direct named-root strength grades written “of” / “out of” | Direct objective statements support observed grades below five, including decimals; five is normal. Negation, question, alternative attribution and opposite-side radiculopathy require review in this new route. Legacy slash-grade gaps remain. | Iteration 5: three positives, twelve negatives |

See [exp-002 REPORT.md](../../autoloop/exp-002/REPORT.md) and
[LOG.md](../../autoloop/exp-002/LOG.md) for hypotheses, prospective repro failures,
paired labels, commits, unsafe intermediate evidence and the self-audit.
The complete statement grammar is deliberately narrow and does not interpret
arbitrary clinical prose or reconcile episodes across sentences.

## Rejected attempt and development evaluation

The spelled-out non-steroidal anti-inflammatory class expansion was reverted:
although its initial pairs, suite and corpus passed, later headache-therapy
attribution challenges produced three false PENDING_VERIFICATION. No improvement
is credited to that candidate. All fourteen inputs from the rejection remain in
the experiment's aggregate; eleven negatives are executable nonpassing guards.

Across **all 69 authored cases** (15 positives / 54 negatives), original-engine
versus final exact status improves **41/69 → 64/69**. Twelve missed passes become
correct PENDING_VERIFICATION, seven become correct NOT_READY, four become correct
NEEDS_REVIEW; no previously correct authored status regresses. False pending
stays **0/54 negatives**. Three positive misses and two wrong negative abstentions
from the rejected class-name family remain. All 55 accepted pairs are exact.
Abstention decreases **100% → 72.46%**, against **65.22% expected**.

The canary always returns CANNOT_DETERMINE: **41/69 exact**, zero false pending,
15 missed passes and 100% abstention. Its zero false pending demonstrates why
that metric alone cannot establish improvement. The unchanged corpus remains
**52/52 exact**, zero false pending; its canary is 30/52 exact with seven missed
passes. The exp-001 expanded 47-case report remains 45/47 exact with two documented
pre-existing false pending results. These development regressions are not held-out
validation and do not establish general clinical-language accuracy.

Held-out evaluation: exp-002 set (36 cases, not visible during development) unchanged at 0 false PENDING_VERIFICATION with no recall gain; prior exp-001 held-out set improved 20 → 23 exact with 0 false PENDING_VERIFICATION. Drug-name → class mapping is out of scope pending an owner decision on an auditable drug list.

That held-out result was supplied by the owner; its cases were not inspected or
rerun during release preparation. In particular, authored recall improvements
are not evidence of recall gains on the exp-002 held-out set.

## Contract, tests and remaining safety pathways

Thirteen new executable contract examples document day/month/word durations,
short/improved courses and named-root word strength grades, including normal,
negated and opposite-side cases. Earlier examples, assertions, expected statuses
and strict markers remain unchanged. The only existing test edits are hardcoded
engine/app version assertions in `test/test_api.py` and `test/test_v210_safety.py`.
No new owner-approved label change or (a)/(b) setup rewrite occurs in this release;
exp-001's approved rewrites remain documented in [v2.3.0](v2.3.0.md).

Fresh validation: **1202 passed, 10 xfailed**; `make lint` passes. Compared with
exp-002's 1189 / 10, the release adds thirteen passing contract examples. All ten
strict xfails remain: four contract examples (unrelated therapy, neither/nor,
same-sentence date borrowing and the disputed negated-score False expectation),
two cross-sentence temporality checks, generic analgesic duration and three H1(a)
next-line/laterality/copy-forward checks. No marker was removed. A negated numeric
score still reviews rather than establishing an observed normal finding.

Human verification remains necessary for READY. Incorrect attestations can still
make unsupported proposals READY; neither/nor and legacy slash-grade attribution
remain false-READY pathways under current verified lumbar governance. CPAP demo
trust independently prevents submission readiness for its same-sentence borrowed
date. These new forms do not repair whole negation or attribution classes.
