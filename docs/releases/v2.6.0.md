# v2.6.0 — broader recall grammar with objective-source safeguards

Engine/app version is **2.6.0**; record `schema_version` remains **2.0.0**.
This release packages exp-004 at `f1412a6`. Release preparation changes docs,
version bookkeeping, executable contract examples and generated version fields.
Extraction logic, drug data, policies, rulebook, clocks, freshness, monitor state
and deployment configuration are unchanged from that experiment.

## Changes released

- Listed-drug courses accept supported reported-use verbs (including “Took”),
  patient subjects, doses/frequencies and duration-first ordering. A whole course
  still needs linked duration and explicit response. Negation, future intent,
  non-patient, indication and unresolved-course guards remain.
- Explicit named-root motor exams accept typed scores without “strength”,
  +/- grades and lower-limb muscle/action names. Tendon-reflex grades distinguish
  normal from changed findings; uncertain or conflicting exams require review.
  The grammar does not infer root distributions from muscle anatomy.
- Additional calendar-valid US numeric, dotted year-first and English month-name
  study dates require direct completed-study linkage. Spelled-out AHI/RDI indices
  include British spelling and space/hyphen variants with explicit measurements.
  New syntax does not generally repair legacy date/abbreviation associations.
- Patient/history attribution or an unperformed exam in a sentence disqualifies
  both typed and legacy motor/reflex evidence. Supported qualifiers include per
  patient, patient reports/states, self-reported, by history, deferred, not
  performed, not examined, unable to assess and limited by refusal. Semicolons
  and decimal grades do not escape this scope. Qualified-only evidence yields
  NOT_DOCUMENTED; conflicting normal/abnormal objective evidence elsewhere yields
  NEEDS_REVIEW with both sources cited. Agreeing objective evidence remains usable.

## Reviewed data, contract and remaining limits

[engine/data/cpb0236_drugs_v1.json](../../engine/data/cpb0236_drugs_v1.json) remains
**owner-approved reviewed data** for local deterministic use, unchanged at 26
records. **ATC codes require human verification against WHO ATC before any external claim.**
Approval does not independently certify each transcription, brand alias,
formulation or indication. Only listed M01A NSAIDs, M03B/M03C muscle relaxants
and N02 analgesics may replace the Footnote 1 class keyword; M03A neuromuscular
blockers and unlisted drugs do not qualify. No new table entry or ontology
service is added, and the table remains outside the existing governance bundle.

Twenty-four new executable contract examples cover all five exp-004 families.
All **67 existing examples**, including **four known-failure objects**, remain
unchanged from f1412a6; the contract now has **91 examples**. The earlier
owner-authorized leading-verb example correction already exists at the release
base and is not a new release-prep label change. Existing test edits only bump
the hardcoded versions in `test/test_api.py` and `test/test_v210_safety.py`.

Fresh validation: **1461 passed, 10 xfailed**; `make lint` passes. The 1437-test
experiment gains 24 contract examples. The ten strict xfails remain: four
contract cases (unrelated numeric-week therapy, neither/nor, same-sentence date
borrowing, disputed negated-strength False expectation), two cross-sentence
temporality cases, generic analgesic duration and three H1(a) review-cue cases.
The disputed numeric score still requires review rather than claiming normality.

Reported drug-course prefixes remain the most overfit-prone recall change:
finite verbs, schedules and whole-course grammar miss valid narrative forms.
Typed exams use bounded anatomy/qualifier vocabularies, not general source
interpretation. Sentence-wide exclusion can abstain when a qualifier describes
another event. Legacy association, broader negation/laterality, cross-sentence
episodes and indication remain open. Human verification is required for READY;
incorrect attestations can still approve unsupported proposals. CPAP demo trust
independently blocks submission readiness for borrowed-date proposals.
