# v2.5.0 — reviewed drug vocabulary with guarded course linkage

Engine/app version is **2.5.0**; record `schema_version` remains **2.0.0**.
This release packages exp-003 at `db49231`. Release preparation changes docs,
version bookkeeping, executable contract examples and generated version fields;
it adds no extraction logic or data entries beyond that experiment. Policies,
rulebook, clocks, freshness, monitor state and deployment configuration stay fixed.

## Owner-approved reviewed drug data

[engine/data/cpb0236_drugs_v1.json](../../engine/data/cpb0236_drugs_v1.json) is
**owner-approved reviewed data**, approved for this local deterministic use.
It has 26 minimal records: generic name, aliases/common US brands, WHO ATC code,
and CPB 0236 Footnote 1 class. **ATC codes require human verification against
WHO ATC before any external claim.** Owner approval is not certification of
each transcription or alias. The file's existing human-review notice is retained.

Only listed M01A NSAIDs, M03B/M03C muscle relaxants and N02 analgesics can replace
the class keyword; M03A neuromuscular blockers are excluded. A non-listed drug
never qualifies by clinical-use inference. The standard-library loader validates
record shape, literal names, unique aliases, ATC prefix and class agreement.
It does not query an ontology service or validate the transcription against WHO
at runtime. The versioned table sits outside the existing governance bundle;
no policy hash, rulebook identity or trust setting is changed by its placement.

Recognition keeps exp-002's isolated whole-course grammar: optional “Completed”,
one listed name, whole days/weeks/months and an explicit “with” response, or the
reverse duration-first form. Duration conversion, response, planned/future,
non-patient, negation, uncertainty and linkage guards remain. Membership does
not establish indication, formulation, adherence or clinical appropriateness.
Unsupported dose/formulation/combination/indication modifiers can prevent capture.
An eligible unresolved drug mention alongside another course requires review
rather than silently disappearing or lending its duration/response.

## Rejected hypothesis and development evidence

The first whole-course-only candidate passed 67 authored pairs, but four later
incomplete-course linkage challenges produced false PENDING_VERIFICATION.
Both changed product modules were immediately reverted to main. The accepted
revision adds the unresolved-context guard. Unsafe source, failing results and
reverted scores remain in [exp-003](../../autoloop/exp-003/REPORT.md); no safety
gain is credited to the rejected candidate. The fixed main engine itself falsely
passed one mixed class/drug challenge, included in the expanded baseline.

Across **74 development cases** (11 positives, 63 negatives), main versus the
accepted experiment improves **49/74 → 74/74 exact**, **0/11 → 11/11 positive
passes**, and **49/63 → 63/63 negative exact statuses**. False pending decreases
**1 → 0**. Abstention decreases **98.65% → 77.03%**, matching **77.03% expected**.
The always-abstain canary is 49/74 exact, zero false pending, 11 missed passes
and 100% abstention. Zero false pending alone cannot demonstrate recall.

The frozen corpus stays **52/52**, zero false pending; its canary is 30/52 and
misses seven positives. Exp-002 stays **64/69**, zero false pending across 54
negatives, with its five documented exact-status misses. Thus all **117 exp-002
and exp-003 authored negatives** are nonpassing. The older expanded exp-001 set
still has two documented false pending results. These finite development checks
are not estimates of performance on clinical notes.

Held-out evaluation
(three sets, none visible during development): 0 false
PENDING_VERIFICATION on all; exact 23→25/41, 15→15/36, 14→15/34. Drug-name
recognition is limited by course-sentence grammar (e.g., a leading verb such
as 'Took' is not yet recognized).

The held-out statement above is supplied by the owner. Those cases were not
available to this release preparation, inspected, added to tests or rerun here.
The counts show modest exact-status changes on two sets and none on the second;
they do not establish broad clinical-language generalization or ATC correctness.

## Contract, counts and remaining limits

Sixteen new executable contract examples cover generic/brand recognition across
all three classes, short and improved courses, future/non-patient/unrelated
context, unlisted drugs, neuromuscular blockers, topical wording, unresolved
course linkage and the leading-verb miss. The existing 51 examples, including
all four known-failure objects, are unchanged. Only hardcoded version assertions
in `test/test_api.py` and `test/test_v210_safety.py` change among existing tests.
No existing label, strict marker or fixture is rewritten.

Fresh validation: **1301 passed, 10 xfailed** (1285 experiment tests plus sixteen
contract additions); `make lint` passes. The ten strict xfails remain: four
contract cases (unrelated numeric-week therapy, neither/nor, same-sentence date
borrowing, disputed negated-strength False expectation), two cross-sentence
temporality cases, generic analgesic duration and three H1(a) review-cue cases.
The disputed numeric score continues to require review, not a normal exam claim.

The isolated course grammar remains the most overfit-prone change. Leading
verbs, dose/frequency prose and many valid modifiers are missed; unresolved drug
context may over-trigger review. Separate-sentence indication/planning and
longitudinal episode linkage remain limited. The table bounds vocabulary; it
does not solve all negation or attribution. Human verification is still required
for READY, and incorrect attestations can still turn unsupported proposals into
READY. CPAP demo trust independently blocks submission readiness for its borrowed
date path; neither/nor and legacy slash-grade attribution remain unsafe classes.
